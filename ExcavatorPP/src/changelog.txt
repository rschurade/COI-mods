Excavator++ — Changelog
================================

v0.1.1
------
- Leftover bucket material now carries over into the next mining job
  instead of being flushed to a truck whenever a designation is
  finished — e.g. the last 2 coal of a coal patch ride along into the
  neighboring limestone designation and the next scoop tops the
  bucket up to a full mixed load. Fixes excavators standing around
  waiting for a truck to collect a few leftover units.
- Only when nothing is left to mine anywhere does the excavator hand
  its remaining bucket content to a truck (instead of the vanilla
  cleanup destroying it).
- On save-load, an excavator frozen waiting for a truck with a
  partial bucket (the vanilla desync, saved mid-cycle) resumes
  mining immediately.

v0.1.0
------
- Initial release: excavators keep mining with a partially filled
  bucket and only unload when the bucket is full, so every truck
  leaves fully loaded and excavators no longer idle waiting for a
  truck to take a few leftover units.
- The bucket is still fully unloaded when a mining job ends (area
  levelled, low fuel, changed product priority), so no material is
  lost.
